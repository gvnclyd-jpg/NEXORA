WELCOME TO NEXORA

Learn first. Trade smarter.

NEXORA is a learning playground for exploring crypto markets without risking real money.

How to use NEXORA

1. Overview: Check your virtual cash, portfolio value, and market summary.

2. Markets: Browse crypto assets, compare displayed prices and daily changes, search assets, and view the chart/timeframes.

3. Practice Trade: Choose an asset, select Buy or Sell, enter a PHP amount, review the estimate, then confirm. You need enough virtual cash to buy or enough holdings to sell.

4. Portfolio: See what you practice-own and how its displayed value changes with the simulator.

5. History & export: Review past practice trades and use CSV export if available to save the history.

6. Headlines: Open market headlines for learning context; headlines are not trade instructions.

7. Reset demo: Reset your practice balance, holdings, and history to start over. This does not reset browser accounts.

Important things to know

🪙 Use only virtual money here. No real crypto is bought or sold.

📡 Quotes may need an internet connection; simulated prices and virtual portfolio changes are not real earnings.

🔒 Demo profiles are stored locally in this browser. They are not secure online accounts, passwords are not verified securely, and profiles do not sync across devices.

🧠 Learn patiently: compare changes over time, understand risk, and never treat a chart or headline as a guarantee.

NEXORA is an educational paper-trading simulator, not financial advice. All balances and trades are simulated.
